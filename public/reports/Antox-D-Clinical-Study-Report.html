1. Study Synopsis & Design

Study Title
A randomised, double-blind, parallel-arm clinical trial to assess the efficacy and safety of a Health supplement (Antox-D liquid) in participants with type 2 diabetes mellitus.
Trial Registry
Clinical Trials Registry - India (CTRI) No.: CTRI/2025/10/095664 [Registered on: 06/10/2025]
Study Design
Randomized, double-blind, parallel-arm, interventional, prospective, Phase II clinical trial.
Treatment Groups
Group A (Test Arm): Antox-D liquid (10 ml twice daily, 1 hr before lunch and dinner with water) + Standard OHAs (n=50 completers).
Group B (Placebo Arm): Placebo liquid (10 ml twice daily, 1 hr before lunch and dinner with water) + Standard OHAs (n=52 completers).
Duration
90 Days primary intervention period, followed by 90-Day extension phase (Day 91 to Day 180).
Investigators & Sites
1. Dr. Anuja Mukesh Phatak, Omkar Multispeciality Hospital, Ravet, Pune, MH - 412101.
2. Dr. Dhyaneshwar Manwatkar, Care Multispeciality Hospital, Wagholi, Pune, MH - 412207.
Inclusion Criteria
Age 30 to 65 years, T2DM on oral hypoglycemic agents (biguanides + sulfonylureas), HbA1c between 7.0% and 9.0%, BMI < 30 kg/m².

4. Safety, Organ Function & Tolerability (Tables 14-17)

  • Complete Blood Count (CBC): Total leukocyte counts, neutrophils, lymphocytes, RBC, platelets, and hemoglobin remained strictly within healthy normal physiological ranges.
  • Liver Function Test (LFT): Bilirubin, SGOT (28.30 U/L), SGPT (33.00 U/L), Alkaline Phosphatase, and Total Proteins demonstrated zero hepatotoxicity.
  • Renal Function Test (RFT): Blood Urea Nitrogen, Serum Creatinine (0.85 mg/dL), and Glomerular Filtration Rate (GFR: 149.60 ml/min) showed healthy renal filtration and no nephrotoxicity.
  • Vital Signs: Blood pressure (Systolic: ~122 mmHg, Diastolic: ~78 mmHg), Pulse rate (~78 bpm), and Body Temperature showed zero systemic instability.
  • Adverse Events: ZERO (0) Adverse Drug Reactions (ADRs) or Serious Adverse Events were observed or reported.
  • Compliance & Tolerability: 100% compliance rate and a score of 03/03 (Excellent Tolerability) across all participants.

6. Official Study Conclusion

"The findings of this Phase II randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trial demonstrate that Antox-D liquid serves as a safe, potent, and highly effective adjunctive nutraceutical intervention in patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus. Significant improvements were achieved in HbA1c reduction (p = 0.0002), fasting and postprandial glucose regulation, profound reversal of insulin resistance (HOMA-IR reduction of 16.49% at Day 90 and 63.18% at Day 180), significant lowering of LDL cholesterol (p = 0.0006), reduction in metabolic syndrome severity (p = 0.027), weight management, and enhancement of overall diabetes-related quality of life — with 100% tolerability and zero adverse events."
